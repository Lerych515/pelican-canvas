Full-scope work

A refit should feel like one interior

When multiple seating areas are worn, dated or poorly coordinated, piecemeal repairs can make the boat look even more fragmented. A larger restoration lets us control the design across the vessel.

We consider seat geometry, foam condition, panel proportions, stitching, color transitions and how each area relates to the next.

  • Complete cockpit and bow seating restoration
  • Helm and captain’s seating
  • Sunpads, lounges, bolsters and backrests
  • Foam rebuilding and reshaping
  • Coordinated material and color selection
  • Custom panel layouts and decorative stitching

Where the value comes from

Consistency

Materials, panel language and stitching are coordinated across multiple seating areas so the interior reads as one design.

Better structure

Old foam and tired shapes are corrected where needed instead of hiding structural problems under new material.

Restraint

Premium does not mean adding decoration everywhere. The goal is to improve the boat without making the interior look overworked.

Designed around the vessel

We work on quality center consoles, dual consoles, family boats and yachts. Vessel size alone does not define a premium project — the scope, expectations and desired result do.

Interior restoration

Coordinated replacement and redesign of worn or dated seating across multiple areas.

Foam & shaping

Correction of tired foam, weak edges and seat geometry where the underlying structure limits the finished result.

Material direction

Marine vinyl, fabrics, thread and details selected around the boat, exposure and design rather than a one-material-fits-all sales pitch.

Plan the full upholstery scope

Full interior work consumes significant design, patterning, fabrication and installation time. We coordinate materials, patterning and finish across the seating areas, with each project quoted for its actual scope.

Every project is reviewed individually based on vessel, scope, materials, foam condition, access and complexity.
