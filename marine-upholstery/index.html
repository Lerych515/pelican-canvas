More than reupholstery

Restore the interior as a complete design

Premium marine upholstery is not just new material over old foam. Seat geometry, foam structure, panel proportions, stitching, transitions and material behavior all affect the finished interior.

Pelican Canvas rebuilds marine seating around the boat itself. The goal is a cleaner fit, better comfort and a finished result that looks intentional — not like an aftermarket repair.

  • Helm seats and captain’s chairs
  • Cockpit seating and lounge cushions
  • Sunpads, bolsters and backrests
  • Foam rebuilding and reshaping
  • Multi-panel custom designs and decorative stitching
  • Complete boat interior restoration

The difference is underneath the vinyl

A premium interior starts with structure and proportion. New vinyl alone cannot correct tired foam, poor shaping or weak panel design.

Fit & geometry

Patterns are developed around the actual seat geometry so seams, corners and transitions land where they belong.

Foam & comfort

Foam is evaluated, rebuilt or reshaped where needed. Comfort and crisp upholstery lines depend on what is underneath.

Design & proportion

Panel size, stitching, piping, color transitions and visual weight are treated as part of the complete boat interior.

Materials chosen for the project — not the sales pitch

South Florida heat, UV, salt and moisture are hard on marine interiors. We select premium marine vinyl, fabrics, foam, thread and hardware according to the boat, exposure, design and expected use.

  • Premium marine vinyl and marine fabrics
  • UV-resistant marine thread
  • Marine-grade foam
  • Stainless marine hardware where required
  • Materials selected for shaping, feel, exposure and long-term use

Marine upholstery questions

Do you do complete boat interior restorations?

Yes. Complete interior restoration is a core part of our work when the project calls for coordinated seating, foam correction and design updates.

Can you reupholster only one seat?

Sometimes. Selective work depends on the seat, existing material condition, color matching and whether the finished piece can integrate properly with the rest of the boat.

Do you rebuild foam?

Yes. Foam condition and geometry are evaluated because clean upholstery and comfort depend on the structure underneath.

Do you offer economy seat recovering?

Pelican Canvas focuses on custom marine upholstery and restoration where fit, materials, design and finish matter. We are not positioned as a lowest-price recovering service.

What areas do you serve?

Hollywood, Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Hallandale Beach, Aventura, Pompano Beach, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach and surrounding South Florida.

How do I start?

Send the boat make, model, year, location, project scope and several photos by text, WhatsApp or email for an initial project review.
