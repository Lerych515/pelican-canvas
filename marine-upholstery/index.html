What premium marine upholstery requires

A good result is more than new vinyl. The shaping, foam, seams, panel balance and material choices must work together.

Cleaner fit

Patterns and panels are developed around the actual seat geometry instead of forcing a generic shape onto the boat.

Marine-grade materials

Premium marine vinyl, UV-resistant thread, marine foam and hardware are selected for Florida conditions and real use.

Controlled details

Stitching lines, transitions, piping and panel proportions are handled as part of the complete interior design.

Marine upholstery questions

Can you reupholster only part of a boat interior?

Yes. The right scope depends on wear, color matching, foam condition and how the refreshed section needs to relate to the rest of the boat.

Do you rebuild foam as well as replace vinyl?

Yes. Foam condition and shaping are reviewed because comfort and clean upholstery lines depend on the structure underneath.
