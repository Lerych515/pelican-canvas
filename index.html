Materials We Trust

Built around premium marine materials

In South Florida, materials matter. Heat, UV, salt air and everyday use expose weak upholstery fast. That is why we use Sunbrella Horizon Marine Vinyl for seating projects where premium feel, durability and long-term appearance matter most.

  • Sunbrella Horizon Marine Vinyl
  • Sunbrella and WeatherMax marine fabrics
  • UV-resistant marine thread
  • Marine-grade foam
  • Stainless marine hardware

Better materials help the boat look sharper, feel better and age more gracefully in Florida sun and salt.

Why we like Sunbrella Horizon

  • Premium feel for marine seating
  • UV and fade resistance
  • Weather, mold and mildew resistance
  • Resistance to microbial pinking
  • Easy to clean
  • 4-way stretch and recovery for cleaner upholstery shaping
