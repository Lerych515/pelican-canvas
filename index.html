Custom Marine Work in Hollywood, Florida

Pelican Canvas is an independent marine upholstery and custom canvas shop based in Hollywood, Florida, serving boat and yacht owners across South Florida.

Premium craftsmanship

The work should improve the whole boat

We look beyond the surface material. Seat geometry, foam, panel balance, stitching, tension, hardware and how the boat is actually used all affect the result.

That same approach applies to canvas: a full cover or enclosure should look intentional, protect properly and work with the boat instead of fighting it.

  • Marine upholstery and complete interior restoration
  • Custom full boat covers and targeted protective covers
  • Bimini tops, shades and enclosures
  • Foam rebuilding and reshaping
  • Premium materials selected for South Florida conditions

Materials Selected for the Project

There is no single “best” material for every boat. The right choice depends on exposure, geometry, feel, maintenance and how the vessel is used.

Marine upholstery materials

Premium marine vinyl, marine fabrics, UV-resistant thread and foam selected for the seating design and environment.

Marine canvas

Sunbrella, WeatherMax and other marine fabrics selected according to the cover, shade or enclosure application.

Construction details

Reinforcement, hardware, attachment points and access are planned into the project instead of added as an afterthought.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a quote?

Send the boat make, model, year, location, project scope and several clear photos by text, WhatsApp or email for an initial project review.

Do you offer economy upholstery or universal covers?

No. Pelican Canvas focuses on custom fabrication and restoration where fit, materials and finish matter.

Do you work on center consoles and family boats, or only yachts?

Both. We work on center consoles, dual consoles and family boats, with the scope reviewed for each vessel. We also take on larger yacht and interior restoration work.

What areas do you serve?

Hollywood, Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Hallandale Beach, Aventura, Pompano Beach, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach and surrounding South Florida.

Ready to improve your boat?

Send the boat details and photos. We will review the scope and discuss the next step.
