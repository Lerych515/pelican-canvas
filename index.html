Common problems

Tired of bad upholstery?

Cheap-looking stitching, vinyl that overheats, worn cushions, loose covers and repairs that never really match the boat. We fix that — properly.

Crooked stitching

Uneven seams make the entire boat feel lower-end, even when the hull and hardware are beautiful.

Sun-beaten materials

South Florida heat destroys weak vinyl and cheap thread fast. Marine work has to be built for UV and salt from day one.

“Repair” look

Owners do not want a patch job that screams patch job. They want clean, intentional work that belongs on the boat.
