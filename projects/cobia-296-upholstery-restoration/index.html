2015 Cobia 296 Full Upholstery Restoration

Complete seating reupholstery by Pelican Canvas — from the bow cushions to the helm chairs and folding transom bench.

Project overview

One finish.
Every seating area.

Pelican Canvas completed a full seating upholstery restoration on this 2015 Cobia 296. The project brings the bow, forward console seat, twin helm chairs and folding transom bench together in a coordinated cream and taupe finish.

Each area has its own shapes and panel layout. Matching colors, carefully placed seams and fitted edges give the seating a consistent appearance throughout the boat.

The upholstery details

A consistent look,

The finished interior combines curved bow cushions, sculpted helm chairs and the straight lines of the transom bench. The shared palette and coordinated panel shapes connect these different seating areas.

The photographs show the result: fitted corners, defined seams and a complete seating layout that works together visually.

Restoration scope

  • Bow cushions, backrests and bolsters
  • Twin helm chairs, armrests and seat bolsters
  • Forward console cushion and backrest
  • Folding transom bench and backrest
  • Coordinated colors and panel detailing
