2015 Cobia 296 Full Upholstery Restoration

A complete seating restoration built around clean fit, balanced panel lines and a premium marine finish that belongs on the boat.

Project overview

A full refresh without losing the Cobia character

This 2015 Cobia 296 received a complete seating reupholstery focused on one goal: make the whole cockpit and bow read as one finished interior.

The restoration keeps the original Cobia design language while tightening the fit, cleaning up the panel transitions and giving the seating a more refined, current appearance.

  • Bow seating reupholstery
  • Helm seats and bolsters
  • Forward console seating
  • Aft and transom seating
  • Fold-out seating surfaces
  • Cobia embroidered logo details retained in the finished design

Where the quality shows

On a 29-foot center console, every seat is visible at once. Inconsistent shaping or seam placement immediately makes the interior feel pieced together.

Fit & shaping

Each cushion is shaped and tensioned so the upholstery follows the seat geometry instead of fighting it.

Consistent panel lines

Color breaks, inserts and seams are balanced across the bow, helm and aft seating so the design reads as one system.

Finished as one interior

The goal is not a collection of newly covered cushions. It is a complete interior that looks intentional on the boat.

Premium marine upholstery

Custom work is judged in the details

Marine upholstery has to survive sun, moisture and regular use, but durability alone is not enough on a quality boat. Pattern development, foam shape, seam placement, tension and finishing all affect the final result.

Pelican Canvas approaches a full restoration as a complete design and fabrication project, not a quick cosmetic recover.
