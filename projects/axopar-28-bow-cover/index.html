Project overview

Coverage shaped around the bow

Pelican Canvas made this custom bow cover for an Axopar 28 in South Florida. The cover closes off the forward seating area and follows the shape of the bow, with the rails and hardware accounted for in the fit.

The finished cover is shown here installed on the boat. The light tan canvas follows the bow perimeter, and the edge is fitted around the rail layout rather than covering a simple rectangular area.
