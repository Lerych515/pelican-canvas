Materials selected for South Florida

Florida is hard on marine canvas. UV exposure, heat, heavy rain, humidity and salt air expose weak materials and poor construction quickly.

Depending on the application, Pelican Canvas works with premium marine fabrics including Sunbrella and WeatherMax, UV-resistant thread and marine-grade hardware. Material selection is based on the boat, how it is stored and how the cover will actually be used.

  • Sunbrella and WeatherMax marine fabrics
  • UV-resistant marine thread
  • Marine-grade hardware
  • Reinforcement at high-load areas
  • Material and construction matched to the application

Custom boat cover questions

Do you make custom full boat covers?

Yes. Each full cover is patterned specifically for the vessel rather than adapted from a universal template.

Do you make covers for center console boats?

Yes. Center consoles and dual consoles are a major part of our custom canvas work, including full covers and targeted protective covers.

What areas do you serve?

Hollywood, Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Hallandale Beach, Aventura, Pompano Beach, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach and surrounding South Florida.

Can you copy my old boat cover?

We can inspect it, but we do not automatically reproduce a poor fit. When necessary, we pattern the boat to produce a cleaner result.

Do you sell universal or economy boat covers?

No. Pelican Canvas focuses on custom-patterned marine canvas and individually quoted work for owners who value fit, materials and finish.

How do I get a quote?

Send the boat make, model, year, location and several photos through WhatsApp. We review the project before scheduling an on-site measurement.
