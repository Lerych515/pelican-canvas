A cover should look fitted, not improvised

Loose fabric, weak tension and generic shapes make a boat cover harder to handle and less effective. Pelican Canvas develops each cover around the boat, the hardware and the way it will be used.

The goal is practical protection with clean tension, controlled lines and marine materials suited to sun, rain, storage and regular handling.

  • Custom full boat covers
  • Center console and dual console covers
  • Seat, helm and cockpit covers
  • Shades and protective canvas
  • Marine enclosures and custom canvas work

Materials and details matter

South Florida sun, rain, humidity and salt air expose weak canvas work quickly. Material selection and construction details are matched to the boat and the way the cover will be used.

  • Sunbrella and WeatherMax marine fabrics
  • UV-resistant marine thread
  • Stainless marine hardware
  • Reinforcement at high-load areas
  • Attachment and access planned into the pattern

Custom boat cover questions

Are the covers made specifically for each boat?

Yes. Our covers are custom-patterned around the actual boat, its equipment and the way the cover needs to be used.

Do you make covers for center consoles and dual consoles?

Yes. Full covers and targeted protective covers can be developed for center consoles, dual consoles and other boat configurations.

Can you make shades and enclosures?

Yes. Pelican Canvas builds custom shades, protective canvas and marine enclosures in addition to boat covers.
