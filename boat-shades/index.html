What we review before quoting

  • Boat make, model and location
  • Bow or aft area to shade
  • Hard-top or T-top shape and attachment areas
  • Existing poles, mounts and hardware
  • Seating access and intended use

Canvas is patterned to the selected hardware layout. Pole and mounting work can be included when required.

Materials, coverage and practical limits

Fabric is selected for the shade application, UV exposure, water behavior and the coverage you want. We work with marine fabrics including Sunbrella and WeatherMax, with reinforcement and thread chosen for the construction.

Removable shade

Storage, installation and removal are part of the layout discussion. Tell us how often you expect to set the shade up.

Wind and underway use

Operating limits depend on the poles, mounting system, fabric span and conditions. Do not assume a shade can remain deployed at speed; discuss the intended use before fabrication.
