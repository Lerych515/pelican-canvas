Pelican Website Analytics

Website performance reporting for Pelican Canvas.

What this application does

Pelican Website Analytics is an internal reporting tool operated by Pelican Canvas. It imports reports for pelican-canvas.com from Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console into a designated, access-controlled Google spreadsheet.

Pelican Canvas uses these reports to understand website traffic, search visibility, useful pages and visitor actions, and to plan improvements to its website and content.

This public page describes the application. Reports are available to the business owner and people the owner authorizes through Google account and spreadsheet sharing settings.

Google data used

  • Google Analytics 4: daily users, sessions, engagement, page views, traffic sources, landing pages, event counts, key events and reported revenue.
  • Google Search Console: site property information, search queries, page URLs, clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position.

The application reads these reports to prepare historical and daily business summaries. It does not change the source Analytics or Search Console properties.

Access and operation

The owner authorizes the application through Google. The collector runs in Google Apps Script, writes reports and sync status to the configured Google Sheet, and uses a daily scheduled trigger.

Google permissions cover reading Analytics and Search Console data, reading and writing spreadsheets, connecting to Google's reporting APIs, and managing the script's scheduled execution. The spreadsheet permission is broader than one file; the collector is configured to write to the designated reporting spreadsheet.

There is no public account registration on this page. This application is intended for Pelican Canvas's own reporting.
